# Repository Communication Privacy Notice

Last reviewed: 12 September 2026  
Next scheduled review: 12 September 2027, or earlier if the processing changes

This notice covers personal information handled by the Universal Ontology project through
private security reports, private Code of Conduct reports, privacy requests, and
related project-governance correspondence. It covers repository communication channels only; it does not describe website
analytics, application telemetry, or processing by deployed ontology services.

## Controller and contact

The controller for the communication channels covered by this notice is
[HADDEN INDUSTRIES LTD](https://data.companieshouse.gov.uk/doc/company/07862561),
registered in England and Wales under company number 07862561.

For questions about this notice or to exercise a data-protection right, email
`privacy@haddenindustries.com`. No separate data-protection officer contact is
published for this project; the privacy role address is the contact point.

## What information we may receive

Depending on what a reporter provides and what is necessary to handle it, the
project may process:

- names, usernames, email addresses, affiliations, and contact preferences;
- the report, correspondence, attachments, witness information, and contextual
  evidence;
- affected revisions or versions, technical environments, reproduction information,
  logs, timestamps, network identifiers, and vulnerability details;
- moderation, investigation, remediation, disclosure, and enforcement decisions;
- records of consent, objections, privacy requests, legal restrictions, or
  conflict handling; and
- limited sensitive information supplied by a reporter or present in relevant
  evidence.

Please provide only what is reasonably necessary. Do not send passwords, access
tokens, unrelated production data, unnecessary identity documents, or unrelated
third-party personal information. Redact or replace live data in vulnerability
reproductions wherever practical.

## Purposes and lawful bases

HADDEN INDUSTRIES LTD processes ordinary personal information where necessary to:

- receive, authenticate, triage, investigate, and respond to security reports;
- protect the project, its consumers, project infrastructure, and community;
- coordinate remediation, disclosure, moderation, and proportionate enforcement;
- prevent repeated abuse or circumvention of an active enforcement decision;
- maintain accountable records, handle privacy requests and complaints, and
  establish, exercise, or defend legal claims; and
- comply with an actual legal or regulatory obligation.

For project security and community-governance handling, the ordinary basis is the
controller's legitimate interests under UK GDPR **Article 6(1)(f)** in maintaining
a secure software project and safe, accountable collaboration. HADDEN INDUSTRIES
LTD considers necessity, proportionality, the reporter's reasonable expectations,
and the rights and interests of affected people. Where a specific law requires
processing, Article 6(1)(c) (legal obligation) applies to that processing instead.

The project does not rely on consent as the routine basis for receiving or
investigating a report, and it performs no solely automated decision-making or
profiling with legal or similarly significant effects.

## Special-category and criminal-allegation information

A conduct or security report can unexpectedly contain special-category personal
data or allegations about criminal conduct. Reporters should omit that material
unless it is strictly necessary.

Secure receipt does not create a blanket entitlement to use such information.
Before substantively using special-category or criminal-offence data, HADDEN
INDUSTRIES LTD will document the applicable Article 6 basis and the additional
Article 9, Article 10, or UK Data Protection Act 2018 condition required for the
specific purpose. Article 9(2)(f) may apply where processing is genuinely
necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims. Other
conditions are assessed case by case rather than presumed. If no applicable
condition exists, the project will minimize, securely delete, or—where
appropriate—refer the material without using it to determine the project report.

## Access and recipients

Maksym Shostak is the sole ordinary security recipient and Community Moderator
under the approved reporting arrangements. Access uses individually authenticated,
least-privilege accounts rather than shared passwords. Report information is
restricted on a need-to-know basis.

If Maksym Shostak is named in or materially conflicted by a conduct report, he
will not adjudicate it alone. The report remains restricted while a separately
governed handler or applicable external legal/platform process is identified.

Information may be disclosed only where necessary to:

- the reporter and people directly involved in investigation or remediation;
- a privately appointed independent handler, professional adviser, insurer, law
  enforcement body, regulator, court, or platform where applicable;
- GitHub for repository activity and private security-advisory handling; and
- Google as the provider of the company-controlled role mailboxes.

GitHub and Google may act as processors, service providers, or independent
controllers for particular platform activities under their own applicable terms.
The project does not publish private report contents as release evidence.

## International transfers

GitHub, Google, or another necessary recipient may process information outside
the United Kingdom. HADDEN INDUSTRIES LTD relies on the recipient's applicable
contractual and data-processing terms and an available UK transfer mechanism—such
as UK adequacy regulations, the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the
UK Addendum to approved standard contractual clauses—as applicable to the actual
transfer. Contact `privacy@haddenindustries.com` for information about the
safeguard relevant to a particular report.

## Retention and deletion

The approved retention schedule is:

| Record                                                         | Normal retention                                            |
| -------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Discarded spam, duplicates, and out-of-scope submissions       | Deleted within 90 days after closure                        |
| Ordinary closed security and conduct case correspondence       | 24 months after closure                                     |
| Minimal record needed to enforce an active project restriction | Life of the enforcement action plus 12 months               |
| Material subject to a legal hold or specific legal obligation  | Only as long as the applicable claim or obligation requires |
| Public, non-personal advisories and release-integrity records  | Retained as project history                                 |

Copies may persist for a limited additional period in provider backups according
to the provider's controlled backup lifecycle. Data is deleted or anonymized
earlier when it is no longer necessary, unless preservation is required for an
active report, safety reason, legal claim, or legal obligation.

## Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, individuals may have rights to:

- be informed and obtain access to their personal information;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
- request erasure or restriction;
- object to processing based on legitimate interests;
- receive portable information where the legal conditions apply; and
- complain about the handling of a request.

The **right to object** to processing based on legitimate interests is brought to
your attention explicitly. A right may be limited where an applicable exemption,
another person's rights, security needs, or the establishment, exercise, or
defence of legal claims requires continued processing. Send requests to
`privacy@haddenindustries.com`. The project may request proportionate information
to verify identity and locate the relevant record.

You may also complain to the UK supervisory authority, the
[Information Commissioner's Office](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/data-protection-complaints/).

## Security incidents and review

The company uses restricted access, individual authentication, MFA where the
provider supports it, and data-minimization practices for these channels. A
suspected personal-data breach is contained, assessed, documented, and notified
to affected people or the Information Commissioner's Office when applicable law
requires it.

This notice is reviewed at least annually and before a materially different use,
recipient, provider, transfer arrangement, or retention rule begins. A changed
notice does not silently authorize an incompatible new purpose for information
already collected.
